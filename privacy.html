Privacy policy

DRP’s Ecosystem · last updated 7 October 2026

DRP’s Ecosystem is a personal Android app used by one person, its developer, on the developer’s own phone. It is not distributed to anyone else.

What the app accesses

  • Gmail, through Google sign-in with the gmail.modify permission: to read mail, apply labels, archive, and send replies that the owner approves with a tap.
  • On-device data the owner grants: notifications, SMS, files and photos.

Where data goes

  • All data stays on the phone, in an encrypted local database.
  • Gmail data is exchanged only between the phone and Google’s Gmail service. It is not sent to any other server, including the developer’s.
  • No analytics, advertising, tracking or crash reporting. No data is sold or shared.
  • AI features run on the phone. Message text is never sent to a cloud AI service.

Google user data

The app’s use and transfer of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements. Gmail data is used only to provide the app’s features to its owner and is not used for advertising or to train models outside the phone.

Control and deletion

  • Access can be revoked at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions.
  • Uninstalling the app deletes everything it stored on the phone.

Contact

Questions: open an issue at github.com/drprockz/drp-site.
