DRP’s Ecosystem

A private Android app for one person.

DRP’s Ecosystem brings messages, documents and photos on its owner’s phone into one encrypted, on-device store. An on-device model helps sort messages, draft replies and organise files. Nothing is sent, moved or deleted without a tap.

It is not published on any app store and is not offered to the public. Sign-in with Google is used only so the owner can read and send their own Gmail from the app.
